## Analysis — what this business needs to operate

_This section is our reasoning about a studio with this service mix. It is not a claim about how Neon Minds actually operates._

1. **Mixed project shapes.** A brand film (weeks, large crew, story development) and a social reel (days, one shooter, many variants) share the same lifecycle but very different durations, crews and deliverable counts. The system must handle both without separate tools.
2. **Deliverable fan-out.** One shoot typically produces a master plus cut-downs and vertical versions for Instagram/YouTube. Deliverables, not projects, are what clients approve, so deliverables and revision rounds need to be first-class.
3. **Client approval is the main source of delay.** Review rounds and approvals sit outside the studio's control. Pending approvals must be visible and aged.
4. **Two physical bases.** Kozhikode and Chennai imply shoots, crew and equipment in two cities. Equipment and crew availability must be location-aware.
5. **Shoot days are expensive and fixed.** Crew, equipment, location and client must align on one date. Double-booking a camera or a DOP is a real operational risk.
6. **Small multi-role team.** A young studio (since 2025) is likely to have people who direct, shoot and edit. Workload must be visible per person across all projects.
7. **AI-integrated workflows are part of the positioning.** An internal system for this studio should itself demonstrate AI assistance — with human confirmation for anything that changes data.
8. **Inbound sales via website/WhatsApp.** The contact form collects a project type; the sales pipeline should start from that same project-type vocabulary.

## Implications for NOVA

- The production lifecycle **Brief → Pre-production → Shoot → Edit → Review → Delivery** is NOVA's operating model (from the product brief and common industry practice). It is not presented as Neon Minds' documented process.
- Project types in NOVA use the site's own vocabulary (the four services plus the contact-form project types).
- Studio locations in NOVA are Kozhikode and Chennai, as stated on the site.
- Brand cues used as design inspiration, not copied: the "cinematic grit with technical precision" tension, the slate/timecode overlay motif, "Beyond Ordinary Visuals".

## Demonstration data policy

All operational data in NOVA is **demonstration data** and is labelled in the UI and seed files.

- **Clients:** fictional companies. Testimonial names from the site are **not** reused as clients, because that would imply a real business relationship.
- **Team:** fictional people with roles typical of a production studio.
- **Projects, shoots, equipment, budgets, revenue:** fictional, sized to be plausible for a small studio, and never presented as Neon Minds' real figures.
- **Portfolio titles** from the site are not used as demo projects.
- The Business lens carries a persistent "Demo data" marker.
